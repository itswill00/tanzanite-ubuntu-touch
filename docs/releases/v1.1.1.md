Ubuntu Touch 20.04 (Focal) / Halium 12 for the **Xiaomi Redmi Note 14 4G** (`tanzanite`, MediaTek Helio G99 / MT6789).

Hotfix from the first on-device test of [1.1.0](v1.1.0.md). Torch and vibration were verified working; **audio did not survive contact with the hardware** — PulseAudio aborted in a loop.

## What was wrong, and why

v1.1.0 shipped the stock `aurisys_config.xml`, whose `AURISYS_SCENARIO_PLAYBACK_SMARTPA` scenario runs `aurisys_demo` + `sia81xx_sixth` (`libsixth.so`, the SiA smart-amplifier userspace DSP). On Ubuntu Touch that chain kills PulseAudio through libhybris: every open of the speaker PCM aborts with `Failed to handle SIGBUS` before a single frame is written — six restarts in 15 seconds, then systemd gives up.

The on-device diagnosis ruled out every hardware suspect first:

| Evidence | Conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| `Ext_Speaker_Amp Switch=on`, `SpkrLeft/Right Sipa Power=1`, full SIPA regmap trace in dmesg | the SiA amplifier is powered and programmed by the kernel — the amp is not the problem |
| `DAC In Mux=Normal Path`, `LOL Mux=Playback`, `RCV Mux=Voice Playback` while playing | the mixer routing is correct |
| `/proc/asound/card0/pcm0p/sub0/status`: `state: PREPARED`, `hw_ptr: 0`, `appl_ptr: 0`, `avail: 2048` | the HAL opens the PCM but **never calls `pcm_write`** — the write path is frozen upstream |
| no kernel `traps:` line at all | the fault is userspace-only (libhybris catches the SIGBUS and aborts) |

An A/B test with the SMARTPA scenario's library list emptied stopped the crash loop (PulseAudio stayed up, `sink.primary_output` appeared) but still produced no sound — an empty Aurisys chain yields no output either. That pinned the fault to the smart-amp userspace DSP chain, not to the sink, the mixer or the amplifier.

## The fix

`AURISYS_SCENARIO_PLAYBACK_SMARTPA` now runs `misoundcrossfade` + `misound` — the chain that `PLAYBACK_NORMAL` already uses and that demonstrably produces output. `libsixth.so` is out of the playback path, so the SIGBUS is gone; the kernel-side `snd_soc_sipa` driver continues to power and program the amplifier. Every other byte of `aurisys_config.xml` remains identical to stock.

Commit: `a8d594e`.

## Known issues

- **Camera** still crash-loops (`camerahalserver` signal 6 every ~5 s, `mali: Failed to get pid pointer`). Next in line.
- The audio policy configuration is still the hand-written one (`version="1.0"`, comma-formatted `samplingRates` on 19 profiles, four stock mixPorts missing). Not implicated in this bug, but a real defect to fix after audio is confirmed working.
